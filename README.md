## The entry

> Tell your Solana wallet agent once, and your SOL is sent, swapped, and sorted before your coffee cools.

It names the wallet agent and Solana outright, promises exactly what the
agent does (send, swap, sort) in plain words a newcomer understands, and
turns it with a fresh, concrete image — your coffee cooling — instead of a
stock crypto line about moons or bags.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` and nothing else.
It validates the line against the competition gate first (UTF-8, 1–140
characters, single line, no leading or trailing white space, at most 20
words).

```sh
python3 main.py                 # writes /output/entry.txt
python3 main.py --self-test     # validates the entry against the gate
```
